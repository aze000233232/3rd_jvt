My Dearest Sol,

Happy 3rd Monthsary, Sol! 🩵

I know I still have a lot to work on, and I can't promise I'll get everything right all the time, but I promise I'll keep trying to be better for myself and for us. I love you so much, mylove mybaby mywife myeverything, and the last thing I ever want is for you to feel like your feelings don't matter to me.

I know we're both going to get busy figuring things out with our lives, and even with the distance between us, I want you to know that you're still the one I want, and you will always be the one I choose. Even on days when we can't talk as much or when life gets in the way, I hope we keep choosing each other and never let the distance or our busy days make us forget what we have. I love you, Sol, and I genuinely hope that one day we'll look back at all these little milestones and realize we made it through everything together, celebrating them until the very end. 🩵
